1. Your site is too slow

If your website takes more than 3 seconds to load, around 40 percent of visitors leave before they even see what you offer. Every slow page is a customer walking to your competitor.

Fix: Compress images, remove heavy plugins, and use proper caching. Speed is the cheapest conversion boost you will ever get.

2. It looks broken on phones

Most of your visitors come from mobile. If buttons are tiny, text overlaps, or the menu does not work on a phone, those visitors are gone in seconds.

Fix: A mobile-first design that is tested on real phones, not just resized on a desktop.

3. No clear way to contact or buy

Visitors should never have to hunt for your phone number, booking button or checkout. Every extra click loses people.

Fix: One clear call to action on every page, visible without scrolling, plus click-to-call and WhatsApp buttons.

4. Google cannot find you

No proper titles, no descriptions, no local setup. If Google does not understand your site, it will not show it, and customers searching nearby will find someone else.

Fix: Basic technical SEO: proper titles, fast pages, and a Google Business Profile done right.

5. It looks outdated, so nobody trusts it

People judge your whole business by your website in under a second. An old, template-looking site quietly tells visitors to go elsewhere.

Fix: A clean, modern design with real photos, real reviews and clear proof you are the right choice.
