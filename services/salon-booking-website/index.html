FAQ

Common questions

How much does a salon booking website cost?

A clean salon site with simple booking starts lower; a full system with staff calendars, deposits and reminders costs more. Message me on WhatsApp with your team size and services for a fixed quote.

Can clients book without calling?

Yes, that is the whole point. Clients pick a service, a staff member and a time slot, and the booking lands in your calendar automatically.

Does it reduce no-shows?

Yes. Automated SMS or WhatsApp reminders before appointments cut no-shows dramatically, and optional deposits protect your time.

Can I manage staff schedules?

Yes, each staff member gets their own calendar with working hours, breaks and days off, and you see everything in one admin view.

Do I need an app too?

Not necessarily. A well-built mobile website handles booking perfectly. If you later want a native app, your booking system can power it.
